How matching works

Each party's manifesto was scored on 13 local issues: 2 for a specific promise, 1 for a general mention, and 0 when nothing was found in this research. Issues you mark as Top priority count double. Each approach answer adds points only for parties whose manifesto states that approach. A party's percentage is its points divided by the most it could have scored on your answers.

Parties marked Partial had less published detail available, so their scores may understate their full plans. Equal scores are listed alphabetically. Only parties contesting in your province, or that could not be ruled out there, are compared.

Voting day basics

When
Wednesday 4 November 2026. Voting stations are open from 07:00 to 21:00.
Bring
One of these: a green barcoded ID book, a smart ID card, or a temporary ID certificate. These are the only documents the IEC lists.
Where
The voting station for the voting district where you are registered. To check it, SMS your ID number to 32810, call 0800 11 8000, WhatsApp 0600 88 0000, or visit elections.org.za ↗.
Ballots
In metros and local councils you get two ballot papers: one for a ward councillor and one for a party. In district council areas you get a third.
At the station
  1. Your ID is scanned at the door and you get a slip confirming you're registered.
  2. Officials cross your name off the voters' roll and mark your left thumbnail with ink.
  3. You receive your ballot papers. Check that each one has a stamp on the back.
  4. In the booth, make one clear X per ballot, next to your choice. Fold the papers.
  5. Put each ballot in the ballot box.
Mistakes
If you spoil a ballot, ask an official for a new one. Photographing your marked ballot is not allowed.
Your ward
To see who is standing in your ward, enter your address at ballotbox.co.za ↗, an independent site (not the IEC). This research did not find a public candidate search on the IEC's own site.
Mayors
Metro mayoral candidates are listed in the Manifesto guide when you pick Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, Western Cape, Eastern Cape or Free State.

What this assistant covers. It answers only from the research behind this page, gathered up to 3 November 2026: the parties' 2026 local-election manifestos, where they are contesting, metro mayoral candidates and voting-day basics. It can't search the internet, it doesn't give opinions, and it won't tell you who to vote for. For anything else, ask the IEC (0800 11 8000, WhatsApp 0600 88 0000) or the parties directly.

Why I made this

I made this tool because I believed it would help people compare the parties and make the right decision for themselves. It is independent: it is not affiliated with, funded by or endorsing any political party, candidate or the Electoral Commission (IEC).

  • Not voting advice. The quiz shows which manifestos say the most about the issues you pick. It does not show which party will deliver, and it is not a recommendation.
  • Check the originals. Information comes from party manifestos and news reports gathered up to 3 November 2026. It may be incomplete or contain errors, and parties can change their plans. Read the manifestos and use official IEC information.
  • Equal treatment. Every party appears in the same format and in alphabetical order, except quiz results, which are ordered by your own answers (ties alphabetical). No party logos or party colours are used; the colours come from the national flag.
  • Please share the link instead of copying content, so people always see the current version.
